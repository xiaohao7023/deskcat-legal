Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

DeskCat does not collect any personal data. All posture analysis happens on your Mac. No video ever leaves your device — there are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs and no third-party tracking.

What runs on your Mac

  • Camera frames are captured only while a posture check runs, analyzed in real time by on-device models (Apple Vision + MediaPipe), and immediately discarded. Frames are not stored, cached or uploaded.
  • A one-time calibration measures your comfortable sitting posture. Only abstract landmark ratios are kept — not images.
  • Posture history and reminders (timestamps and scores) are stored locally in the app container on your Mac.

App Store connection

Purchase and restore of DeskCat Pro goes through Apple's payment system. DeskCat stores only the entitlement result (unlocked or not) on your device, never payment details.

Deleting your data

History can be cleared inside the app at any time. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Children

DeskCat is a general adult productivity app and is not directed at children.
