Common topics

  • Camera not working: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera → enable DeskCat, then relaunch the app from the menu bar.
  • Calibration: Sit the way you want to be reminded back to, then run calibration once. Re-run it any time your chair or desk changes.
  • Restore purchases: Pro is tied to your Apple ID. Use Restore in the app if it doesn't unlock.
